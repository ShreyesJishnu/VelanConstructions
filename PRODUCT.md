# Velan Concast — Product Truth

## What it is

A precast concrete manufacturer in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Products are cast in a
factory and delivered finished to site, replacing weeks of on-site construction with a
delivery and a crane lift.

## Platform

`web`

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + React, decided by the user. Existing repo also serves a verbatim
clone of the previous site at its original `.php` URLs; that clone is reference, not the
product.

## Primary user

**Builders and contractors** sourcing precast units for residential and commercial
projects — the repeat buyer. They arrive knowing roughly what they need (a 10,000 litre
sump, a septic tank for a 40-flat block) and are deciding *who to call*, not *what
precast is*. They care about: capacity/dimensions, lead time, whether it leaks, whether
it can be customised, and whether the supplier can actually deliver at their volume.

Secondary, not designed for first: homeowners buying one tank; industrial and
institutional buyers (STP/ETP, bus shelters, public toilets); architects specifying.

## The job

"I need N units of X by date D — can these people do it, and what will it cost me?"

## Success

**A phone call or a WhatsApp message.** Phone is the real sales channel of this
business. The enquiry form is the after-hours fallback, not the goal. Every screen
should keep a live phone number within reach.

## Mechanism / position

**Single-mould construction.** The tank is cast as one piece rather than assembled from
panels, which is the source of the leak-proof claim and the fast-installation claim.
This is the one thing that is genuinely different and every other benefit descends from
it: heavy steel reinforcement, factory-made precision, low maintenance, no site
construction.

## Product range

Water tanks / sump · Septic tanks · Customized water tanks · STP/ETP · Cabin room ·
Concast house · Concast bus shelter · Concast toilets & urinal units · Trenches ·
Box culverts · Staircases · Traffic control barriers · Compound walls, slabs, drains,
