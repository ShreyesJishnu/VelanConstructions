Concast Toilets

To meet the various requirements of the customers, we are involved in offering a wide assortment of Concrete urinals and toilets. The Concrete Toilet/urinals is a High Quality, transportable, efficient and sanitary variation of a common facility for the elimination of waste. The waste will be deposited through the hole directly to the ground below. A more refined version of the outhouse was the water closet, an indoor facility with a water tank and flushing system that deposited the waste in a cesspit below. These toilets can be installed on-site or in remote locations as per customer's requirements.
