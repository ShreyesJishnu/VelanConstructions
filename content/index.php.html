Velan Concast

We are a precast concrete products manufacturing company in Coimbatore. We are committed to creating top-notch precast concrete products, with the best quality, without compromising on durability and reliability. We believe in offering cutting-edge, engineered precast concrete products to our customers with a safe working atmosphere for every person on the team.

The production unit situated in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India has 2, acres with adequate supply of water and electricity in two different places (Unit-I and Unit-II). The state-of-the-art machinery essential equipment and accommodation for the semi-skilled personnel. The vast area can stockpile requirements of steel, sand, cement etc., so that round the clock work can be executed, ensuring prompt delivery schedules.
