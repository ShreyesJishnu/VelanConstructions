Cabin Room

Cabin Room

Velan concast offers a wide range of precast cabin houses designed by our professional architects and manufactured with a precise and proven process that offers optimal utility value, quality promise, and design flexibility. These cabin houses are available in different standard sizes. This detachable cabin house is made up of 5 individual parts which are assembled at the site. Each wall panel is a precast concrete unit that can be easily assembled together at a remote location. After assembly, the walls are internally and externally coated with long-lasting paint making it not only climate-friendly and weatherproof but also providing better insulating qualities.

Controlled Climate
Concrete precast homes provide a tight thermal building envelope which can reduce a home owner’s heating and cooling bills.
Durability
The use of steel reinforced concrete panels make for a rock solid durable living environment. You can be at peace knowing that you are in a safe and secure home that far exceeds the performance levels of conventional building systems.
Reduced noise pollution
The well-known acoustic properties of concrete produce a significantly quieter environment and reduce noise pollution inside the home.
Straight and true
As the wall panels are factory manufactured the construction is very precise. Other trades repeatedly comment on the ease of working with these buildings. When you start straight you finish straight.
