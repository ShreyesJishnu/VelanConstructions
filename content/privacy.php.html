Privacy Policy

Velan Concast is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you interact with us through our website, WhatsApp, social media platforms, Meta lead forms, phone calls, or any other communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website, we may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and other contact details when you provide it voluntarily through forms, surveys, or other means.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Contact you for installation, service, and support
  • Process quotations, orders, invoices, and project-related communication
  • Improve our product quality and customer experience
  • Send updates, offers, promotions, and relevant information
  • Respond to enquiries on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google, or phone calls
  • Maintain internal records and compliance requirements
  • Provide and deliver our precast products (water tanks, sumps, STP/ETP units, etc.)

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or trade your personal data.

4. Data Protection & Security

We take appropriate measures to protect your information from:

  • Unauthorised access
  • Loss
  • Misuse
  • Alteration or disclosure

This includes secure servers, encrypted storage, controlled access, and restricted sharing.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Opt-out of marketing messages at any time

You can contact us directly to exercise these rights.

6. Data Retention

We store your information only as long as required for:

  • Completing your project
  • Providing after-sales support
  • Marketing purposes — offers, new products, etc.

After this period, data is securely deleted.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website, WhatsApp, or social media may contain links to other sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update this policy occasionally.
  • Any changes will be posted with a revised “Last Updated” date.
